今日英文

> The sooner you deal with a problem, the easier it is to prevent it from getting worse.



中文：
你越早处理一个问题，就越容易防止它变得更糟。

📌 Key Expressions

the sooner..., the easier... — 越早……，就越容易……

deal with a problem — 处理问题；应对问题

prevent A from doing B — 防止 A 做 B

get worse — 变得更糟；恶化

deal with... — 处理；解决；应付
